We are working with the Allen Institute for Brain Science to develop novel methods for analyzing spatial transcriptomic data collected using MERSCOPE technology. Through them, we have access to >300 slices of mouse brain slices across a total of six animals (3 F, 3 M), totalling over 30 million cells across these slices. Using this data, we could either do an exploratory analysis of a specific region/cell type of the mouse brain (i.e. looking at the density of astrocytes in the amygdala, or a question of that sort), or we can do differential analysis between one of three comparisons.

The potential comparative analyses would be looking for hemispheric, sex, or developmental differences between these wild type animals. If hemispheric, we would look for any differences in the left and right hemispheres of 4 P56 day old mice (2 F, 2 M) across 279 genes. The sex based analysis would be using the same 4 P56 mice but comparing between the 2 F and 2 M mice for whole brain differences between the two groups in the same 279 genes. Lastly, the developmental question would have one of two possible approaches. The first would be to look at the 4 P56 animals and 2 P28 animals across 279 genes in the entire brain. The other potential approach would be comparing between 2 P56 animals (1 F, 1 M) and 2 P28 animals (1 F, 1 M) across a total of at least 550 genes across the whole brain.
